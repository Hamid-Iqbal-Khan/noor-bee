568	Where a madd letter (ا و ي) carries the madd sign (~), make the sound longer than a normal madd. If a hamzah comes in the same word (جَآءَ) it is madd muttasil; if the hamzah is in the next word (بِمَآ اُنْزِلَ) it is madd munfasil — both 4 to 5 counts. If a shaddah or sukoon follows the madd (الضَّآلِّيْنَ) it is madd laazim — always a full 6 counts. One count = the time it takes to fold one finger.

643	When you stop on a word at the end of an ayah or when your breath runs out, drop the vowel of the last letter and read it resting. If there is a madd before the last letter (الرَّحِيْمْ, الْعٰلَمِيْنْ), you may stretch it 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping — keep the same length within one recitation.
