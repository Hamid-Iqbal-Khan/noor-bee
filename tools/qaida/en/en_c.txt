444	Past tense (a finished action): the ending tells who did it — ـَ he, ـُوْا they, ـَتْ she, ـْتَ you, ـْتُ I, ـْنَا we. Note: besides simple verbs like خَلَقَ there are other patterns such as قَدَّمَ and اَنْعَمَ; here we only learn the ending.
445	Present tense (an action happening now or to come): the first letter tells who is doing it — يَـ he, تَـ you, اَ I, نَـ we. If it ends in ـُوْنَ, it means many people.

516	These marks appear often in the IndoPak Quran. Standing fatha = fatha + the length of an alif, standing kasra = kasra + the length of a yaa, inverted damma = damma + the length of a waw. Stretch for the length of one alif (about two counts).
