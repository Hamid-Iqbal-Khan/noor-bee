101	Simple meaning
102	The first surah of the Quran, also called 'Umm al-Quran' (the Mother of the Quran). It is recited in every rak'ah of every prayer; it praises Allah and asks for the straight path.
103	This surah tells of Allah's Oneness (tawheed). The Prophet ﷺ said this surah is equal to a third of the Quran.
104	When enemies mocked that the Prophet ﷺ would have no one to carry on his name, Allah comforted him: he has been given a great deal of good.
105	In three ayat, four keys to success: faith, good deeds, urging one another to the truth, and urging one another to patience.
106	This and the next surah are called 'al-Mu'awwidhatayn' (the two surahs of seeking refuge). Before sleeping, the Prophet ﷺ would recite them, blow into his hands and wipe them over his body.
107	The second surah of refuge: seeking Allah's protection from whispers inside us. Reciting it before sleep and in the morning and evening is sunnah.
108	In the year the Prophet ﷺ was born, Abrahah, the ruler of Yemen, came with an army of elephants to destroy the Ka'bah. Allah sent birds that pelted them with stones and the whole army was destroyed.
109	The Quraysh of Makkah were traders: in winter they travelled to Yemen and in summer to Syria. By the blessing of the Ka'bah their journeys were safe, so they are told to worship the Lord of the Ka'bah.
110	Religion is not only prayer: caring for orphans and the poor, avoiding showing off, and giving small help are also part of faith.
111	The chiefs of Makkah offered that the Prophet ﷺ worship their idols for a while and they would worship Allah for a while. This surah came down: there is no bargaining in worship.
112	Good news of the conquest of Makkah and of people entering Islam in crowds. It is said to be the last complete surah revealed.
113	Abu Lahab was the Prophet's ﷺ uncle but his bitter enemy; his wife would put thorns in the Prophet's path. Being a relative does not help without faith.
114	The end of those who mock, backbite and are proud of their wealth.
115	The race to gather more and more makes people forget Allah; every blessing will be asked about.
116	On the Day of Judgement the earth will tell everything; even an atom's weight of good is never lost.
117	The Quran came down on the Night of Power (Laylat al-Qadr). It is sought in the last ten nights of Ramadan, especially the odd nights.
118	Its first five ayat are the very first revelation, which came to the Prophet ﷺ in the Cave of Hira. Its last ayah is an ayah of sajdah.
119	Comfort for the Prophet ﷺ in hard times: with every hardship comes ease.
120	When revelation paused for some days, enemies said his Lord had left him. This surah came down to comfort him.
121	The story of the people of Thamud: they killed the she-camel, a sign from Allah, and were destroyed. Success is in keeping the heart pure.
122	The end of mighty nations such as 'Aad, Thamud and Pharaoh, and good news for 'the soul at peace'.
123	The Prophet ﷺ often recited this surah (and Al-Ghashiyah) in the Jumu'ah and Eid prayers.
124	Long ago some believers were thrown into fiery trenches only because of their faith; Allah tells of the end of the wrongdoers.
125	Scenes of the Day of Judgement. The Prophet ﷺ said: whoever wants to see the Day of Judgement as if with his own eyes, let him read At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq.
126	The Prophet ﷺ was inviting the chiefs of Makkah to Islam when a blind Companion, 'Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum, came to learn the religion. Allah taught that a sincere learner deserves more attention. Later the Prophet ﷺ honoured him greatly.
127	Juz 'Amma begins with this surah: the 'great news' of the Day of Judgement, Allah's blessings, and Paradise and Hell.
128	Respect for the Prophet ﷺ is always required. Tell children that here Allah taught us to value every learner, even if poor or blind.
129	In the time before Islam some people buried baby daughters alive. Islam called this a great sin and gave daughters honour. Tell young children briefly and gently.
130	It mentions the blessings of Paradise. For young children it is enough to say that Paradise has every joy and every good thing.
131	Do not imagine Allah like His creation; He comes in the way that befits His majesty.
132	This surah does not teach us to call anyone names; it tells that enmity towards Allah's Messenger ﷺ ends badly.
