By the morning brightness
and by the night | when | it is still
Not | has left you | your Lord | nor | is He displeased
and surely the Hereafter | is better | for you | than | the first (life)
and soon | will give you | your Lord | and you will be pleased
Did not | He find you | an orphan | and gave you shelter
and He found you | unaware of the way | and guided you
and He found you | in need | and made you free of need
So as for | the orphan | then do not | be harsh with him
and as for | the one who asks | then do not | turn him away
and as for | the blessing | of your Lord | then speak of it
