By the racing horses | panting
then striking sparks | with their hooves
then raiding | at dawn
then raising | thereby | dust
then charging | thereby | into the army
Indeed | man | to his Lord | is very ungrateful
and indeed he | over | that | is himself a witness
and indeed he | in his love | of wealth | is very strong
Does not | he know | when | will be brought out | what | is in | the graves
and will be made known | what | is in | the hearts
Indeed | their Lord | of them | that Day | is fully aware
