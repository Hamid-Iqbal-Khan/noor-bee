By the night | when | it covers
and by the day | when | it shines
and by Him who | created | the male | and the female
Indeed | your efforts | are of many kinds
So as for | whoever | gives | and fears Allah
and believes in | the best
We will make easy for him | the way of ease
But as for | whoever | is stingy | and thinks himself free of need
and denies | the best
We will make easy for him | the way of hardship
and not | will help | him | his wealth | when | he falls
Indeed | upon Us | is guidance
and indeed | to Us belong | the Hereafter | and this world
So I warn you | of a fire | blazing
not | will burn in it | except | the most wretched
who | denied | and turned away
But will be kept away from it | the most God-fearing
who | gives | his wealth | to purify himself
and not | anyone has | with him | any | favour | to be repaid
except | seeking | the pleasure | of his Lord | the Most High
and he will surely soon | be pleased
