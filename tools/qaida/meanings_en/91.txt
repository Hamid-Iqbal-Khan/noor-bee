By the sun | and its morning brightness
and by the moon | when | it follows it
and by the day | when | it shows it
and by the night | when | it covers it
and by the sky | and Him who | built it
and by the earth | and Him who | spread it
and by the soul | and Him who | shaped it well
then showed it | its wrong | and its right
Surely | has succeeded | whoever | purifies it
and surely | has failed | whoever | corrupts it
denied | Thamud | in their transgression
when | rose up | the most wretched of them
Then said | to them | the messenger | of Allah | the she-camel | of Allah | and her drink
But they denied him | and killed her | so destroyed | them | their Lord | for their sin | and levelled them
and not | He fears | its consequence
