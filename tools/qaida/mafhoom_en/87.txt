Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High,
who created and then made everything well,
who set the measure (of all things) and then guided,
who brought out the pasture (for animals),
then turned it into dry, dark stubble.
We will make you recite (the Quran), and you will not forget,
except what Allah wills; surely He knows what is open and what is hidden.
And We will make it easy for you to follow the easy way (of the religion).
So remind, wherever the reminder benefits.
Whoever fears (Allah) will take the reminder,
but the most wretched will avoid it,
who will enter the greatest Fire,
where he will neither die nor (truly) live.
Surely successful is the one who purifies himself,
and remembers the name of his Lord and prays.
But you prefer the life of this world,
while the Hereafter is better and lasts forever.
Surely this is also in the earlier scriptures,
the scriptures of Ibrahim and Musa (peace be upon them).
