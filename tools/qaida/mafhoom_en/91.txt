By the sun and its morning brightness,
and by the moon when it follows it,
and by the day when it shows the sun's brightness,
and by the night when it covers it,
and by the sky and the One who built it,
and by the earth and the One who spread it out,
and by the soul and the One who shaped it well,
then taught it (to know) its wrong and its right:
surely successful is the one who keeps his soul pure,
and surely the one who corrupts it (with sins) has failed.
The people of Thamud denied (their prophet) because of their arrogance,
when the most wretched of them rose up.
Then the messenger of Allah (Salih, peace be upon him) told them: Take care of Allah's she-camel and her turn to drink.
But they denied the messenger and killed the she-camel, so their Lord destroyed them for their sin and levelled them all (to the ground).
And Allah has no fear of what comes after.
