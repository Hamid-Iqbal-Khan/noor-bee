By the sky and the night visitor!
And what will make you know what the night visitor is?
It is the piercing bright star.
There is no soul that does not have a guardian (angel) over it.
So let man see what he was created from.
He was created from a gushing fluid,
coming from between the backbone and the ribs.
Surely Allah is fully able to bring him back,
on the Day all secrets are tested,
when he will have no strength and no helper.
By the sky that returns the rain,
and by the earth that splits open (for plants),
surely this (Quran) is a decisive word,
and it is not a joke.
Surely they (the disbelievers) are plotting,
and I too am planning.
So (O Prophet ﷺ) give the disbelievers time; give them a little time.
