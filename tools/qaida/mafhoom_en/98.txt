Those who disbelieved among the People of the Book and the idol-worshippers were not going to stop (their disbelief) until the clear proof came to them:
a messenger from Allah, reciting purified pages,
in which are upright and true teachings.
And the People of the Book divided only after the clear proof had come to them.
Yet they were commanded only to worship Allah, keeping the religion sincerely for Him, turning to Him alone, to establish prayer and to give zakah — and that is the upright religion.
Surely those who disbelieve among the People of the Book and the idol-worshippers will stay forever in the fire of Hell; they are the worst of all creation.
Surely those who believe and do good deeds are the best of all creation.
Their reward with their Lord is gardens of eternity with rivers flowing beneath them, where they will stay forever and ever. Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him. That is for whoever fears his Lord.
