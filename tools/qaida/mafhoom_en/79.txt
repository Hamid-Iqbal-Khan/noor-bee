By the angels who pull out (the souls of the wicked) harshly,
and by those who draw out (the souls of the believers) gently,
and by those who glide (through the heavens),
then race ahead (to carry out orders),
then arrange affairs (by Allah's command).
On the Day the quaking (earth) quakes,
followed by another (blast),
hearts that Day will be pounding,
and their eyes will be cast down.
They say: Shall we really be returned to our former state?
Even when we have become crumbled bones?
They say: Then that would be a return of great loss!
But it will be only a single shout (the Trumpet),
and at once they will be on open ground.
Has the story of Musa (peace be upon him) reached you?
When his Lord called him in the holy valley of Tuwa:
Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed,
and say to him: Would you like to purify yourself,
and that I guide you to your Lord so that you fear Him?
Then Musa showed him the greatest sign,
but he denied and disobeyed,
then turned his back, striving (against the truth),
then gathered (his people) and called out,
saying: I am your lord, the most high.
So Allah seized him with the punishment of the Hereafter and of this world.
Surely in this is a lesson for whoever fears (Allah).
Are you harder to create, or the sky? Allah built it,
raised its ceiling high and made it even,
darkened its night and brought out its daylight,
and after that He spread out the earth,
brought out of it its water and its pasture,
and fixed the mountains firmly in it,
as provision for you and your cattle.
Then when the greatest disaster (the Day of Judgement) comes,
that Day man will remember what he strove for,
and Hell will be shown to all who see.
So whoever transgressed,
and preferred the life of this world (over the Hereafter),
Hell will surely be his home.
But whoever feared standing before his Lord and held back his soul from evil desires,
Paradise will surely be his home.
They ask you about the Hour: when will it come?
What have you to do with telling its time?
Its (full) knowledge is with your Lord alone.
You are only a warner for whoever fears it.
On the Day they see it, it will seem as if they had stayed (in the world) only an evening or a morning.
