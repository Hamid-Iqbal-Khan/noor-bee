Woe to every person who mocks to the face and finds faults behind the back,
who gathers wealth and keeps counting it.
He thinks his wealth will make him live forever.
No indeed! He will surely be thrown into "al-Hutamah".
And what will make you know what al-Hutamah is?
It is the fire of Allah, kindled,
which reaches up to the hearts.
It will be closed in over them from every side,
in long stretched-out columns.
