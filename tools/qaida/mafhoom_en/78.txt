What are they asking one another about?
About the great news (the Day of Judgement),
about which they disagree.
No indeed! They will soon know.
Again, no indeed! They will soon know.
Have We not made the earth a resting place,
and the mountains as pegs,
and created you in pairs,
and made your sleep for rest,
and made the night a covering,
and made the day a time to earn a living,
and built above you seven strong (heavens),
and made a blazing lamp (the sun),
and sent down pouring water from the rain clouds,
to bring out grain and plants with it,
and gardens thick with trees?
Surely the Day of Decision is an appointed time,
the Day the Trumpet is blown and you come in crowds,
and the sky is opened and becomes gateways,
and the mountains are moved and become like a mirage.
Surely Hell is lying in wait,
a home for the transgressors,
where they will stay for ages.
There they will taste nothing cool and nothing to drink,
except boiling water and pus,
a fitting reward for what they did.
They never expected a reckoning,
and they completely denied Our signs,
and We have counted everything in writing.
So taste (the punishment); We will only increase you in punishment.
Surely for those who fear Allah there is success:
gardens and grapes,
and young companions of equal age,
and cups full to the brim.
There they will hear no idle talk and no lies,
a reward from your Lord, a gift in full measure,
from the Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, the Most Gracious; no one has the power to speak to Him.
On the Day the Spirit (Jibril) and the angels stand in rows, no one will speak except the one the Most Gracious allows, and he will say what is right.
That Day is the truth; so whoever wills, let him take a way back to his Lord.
We have warned you of a punishment that is near, the Day a person will see what his hands have sent ahead, and the disbeliever will say: I wish I were dust!
