By the night when it covers (everything),
and by the day when it shines,
and by the One who created the male and the female:
surely your efforts are of many kinds.
So whoever gives (in Allah's way) and fears Allah,
and believes in the best (Islam),
We will make easy for him the way to ease (Paradise).
But whoever is stingy and thinks himself free of need,
and denies the best,
We will let him go the way to hardship (Hell).
And his wealth will not help him at all when he falls (into the pit).
Surely it is up to Us to show the way,
and surely the Hereafter and this world belong to Us.
So I have warned you of a blazing fire.
Only the most wretched will enter it,
who denied and turned away.
And the most God-fearing will be kept away from it,
who gives his wealth to purify himself,
not to repay a favour anyone has done him,
but only seeking the pleasure of his Lord, the Most High;
and he will surely soon be pleased.
