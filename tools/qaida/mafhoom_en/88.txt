Has the story of the Overwhelming (Day) reached you?
Some faces that Day will be humbled,
toiling and weary,
entering a blazing fire,
given water from a boiling spring to drink.
They will have no food except thorny bushes,
which neither nourish nor satisfy hunger.
And some faces that Day will be joyful,
pleased with their efforts (in the world),
in a high garden,
where they will hear no idle talk.
In it is a flowing spring,
in it are raised couches,
and cups set out,
and cushions lined up in rows,
and carpets spread out.
Do they not look at the camel, how it was created?
And at the sky, how it was raised high?
And at the mountains, how they were set firm?
And at the earth, how it was spread out?
So (O Prophet ﷺ) remind; you are only one who reminds.
You are not there to force them.
But whoever turns away and disbelieves,
Allah will punish him with the greatest punishment.
Surely to Us is their return,
then surely their reckoning is up to Us.
