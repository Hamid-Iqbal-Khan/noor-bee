The Striking Calamity (the Day of Judgement)!
What is the Striking Calamity?
And what will make you know what the Striking Calamity is?
The Day people will be like scattered moths,
and the mountains will be like fluffed-up coloured wool.
Then whoever's scales (of good deeds) are heavy
will be in a happy life.
But whoever's scales are light,
his home will be "Haawiyah" (the deep pit).
And what will make you know what it is?
It is a blazing fire.
