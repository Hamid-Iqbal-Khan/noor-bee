By the horses that race, panting,
then strike sparks with their hooves (on the stones),
then raid at dawn,
then raise clouds of dust,
then charge into the middle of the (enemy) army!
Surely man is very ungrateful to his Lord,
and he himself is a witness to this,
and he loves wealth very strongly.
Does he not know that when what is in the graves is brought out,
and what is in the chests (hearts) is made known,
surely their Lord will be fully aware of them that Day?
