When the earth is shaken with its great shaking,
and the earth brings out its burdens (from inside),
and man says: What is wrong with it?
That Day the earth will tell all its news,
because your Lord will have commanded it.
That Day people will come out in separate groups to be shown their deeds.
So whoever has done an atom's weight of good will see it,
and whoever has done an atom's weight of evil will see it too.
