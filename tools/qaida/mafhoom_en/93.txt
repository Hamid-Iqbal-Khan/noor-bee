By the morning brightness,
and by the night when it is still:
(O Prophet ﷺ) your Lord has not left you, nor is He displeased.
And surely the Hereafter is better for you than this world.
And soon your Lord will give you so much that you will be pleased.
Did He not find you an orphan and give you shelter?
And He found you unaware of the way and guided you,
and He found you in need and made you free of need.
So do not be harsh with the orphan,
and do not turn away the one who asks,
and speak of the blessings of your Lord.
