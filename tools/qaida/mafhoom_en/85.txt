By the sky with its great stars,
and by the promised Day,
and by the witness and what is witnessed:
destroyed were the people of the trench,
the fire full of fuel,
when they sat by it (the fire),
watching what they were doing to the believers.
They hated the believers only because they believed in Allah, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy,
to whom belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth; and Allah sees everything.
Surely those who tortured the believing men and women and then did not repent will have the punishment of Hell and the punishment of burning.
Surely those who believe and do good deeds will have gardens with rivers flowing beneath them; that is the great success.
Surely the grip of your Lord is very strong.
It is He who creates the first time and He will create again,
and He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Loving,
the Owner of the Throne, the Glorious,
who does whatever He wills.
Has the story of the armies reached you,
the armies of Pharaoh and Thamud?
Rather, the disbelievers are busy denying,
and Allah surrounds them from behind.
Rather, this is a glorious Quran,
(written) in the Preserved Tablet.
