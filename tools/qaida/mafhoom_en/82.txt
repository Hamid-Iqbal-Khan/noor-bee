When the sky is split open,
and when the stars are scattered,
and when the seas burst forth,
and when the graves are turned over,
then every soul will know what it sent ahead and what it left behind.
O man! What has deceived you about your Lord, the Most Generous,
who created you, then shaped you, then made you well balanced,
and put you together in whatever form He willed?
No indeed! Rather, you deny the Day of Judgement,
while over you there are guardians (angels),
noble ones, writing (your deeds),
who know whatever you do.
Surely the righteous will be in bliss,
and surely the wicked will be in Hell,
which they will enter on the Day of Judgement,
and they will never be able to escape it.
And what will make you know what the Day of Judgement is?
Again, what will make you know what the Day of Judgement is?
It is the Day no soul can do anything for another soul, and on that Day the whole command is Allah's.
