When the sky is split open,
and listens to its Lord, as it must,
and when the earth is spread out,
and throws out what is inside it and becomes empty,
and listens to its Lord, as it must.
O man! You are working hard on your way to your Lord, and you will meet Him.
Then whoever is given his record in his right hand
will have an easy reckoning,
and will return to his family happy.
But whoever is given his record from behind his back
will call out for death,
and will enter the blazing fire.
He used to be happy and carefree among his family (in the world).
He thought he would never return (to Allah).
Yes indeed! His Lord was always watching him.
So no! I swear by the red glow of sunset,
and by the night and all it gathers,
and by the moon when it becomes full:
you will surely pass from one stage to another.
So what is wrong with them that they do not believe?
And when the Quran is recited to them, they do not prostrate.
Rather, the disbelievers deny,
and Allah knows best what they hide in their hearts.
So give them news of a painful punishment,
except those who believe and do good deeds; for them is a reward that never ends.
