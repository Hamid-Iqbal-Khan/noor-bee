He (the Prophet ﷺ) frowned and turned away,
because a blind man (a Companion) came to him.
And how could you know? Perhaps he would have purified himself,
or taken the reminder, and the reminder would have benefited him.
But the one who thinks himself free of need,
you give your attention to him,
though it is not your duty if he does not purify himself.
And the one who came to you hurrying,
while he fears (Allah),
you give him less attention.
No indeed! This (Quran) is a reminder,
so whoever wills, let him remember it.
It is in honoured pages,
raised high and purified,
in the hands of scribes (angels),
noble and good.
Woe to man! How ungrateful he is!
From what did Allah create him?
From a drop of fluid He created him, then gave him his measure,
then made the way (of life) easy for him,
then gave him death and put him in a grave,
then when He wills, He will raise him up again.
No indeed! He has not yet done what Allah commanded him.
So let man look at his food:
how We poured down water in plenty,
then split the earth open,
then made grain grow in it,
and grapes and vegetables,
and olives and date palms,
and gardens thick with trees,
and fruit and pasture,
as provision for you and your cattle.
Then when the deafening blast (of the Day of Judgement) comes,
that Day a man will flee from his brother,
and from his mother and his father,
and from his wife and his children.
That Day every person will have enough worry of his own to keep him from others.
Some faces that Day will be bright,
laughing and rejoicing.
And some faces that Day will be covered with dust,
covered in darkness.
Those are the disbelievers, the wicked.
