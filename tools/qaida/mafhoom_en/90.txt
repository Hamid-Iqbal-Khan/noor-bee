No! I swear by this city (Makkah),
and (O Prophet ﷺ) you live in this very city,
and by the father (Adam, peace be upon him) and his children:
surely We created man in toil and hardship.
Does he think no one has power over him?
He says: I have spent heaps of wealth.
Does he think no one has seen him?
Have We not given him two eyes,
and a tongue and two lips,
and shown him the two ways (of good and evil)?
Yet he has not attempted the steep path (of good).
And what will make you know what the steep path is?
It is freeing a slave,
or giving food on a day of hunger
to an orphan who is a relative,
or to a poor person in great need,
and then being among those who believe and urge one another to patience and to mercy.
These are the companions of the right hand (the fortunate).
But those who deny Our signs are the companions of the left hand (the unfortunate).
Over them will be a fire closed in on every side.
