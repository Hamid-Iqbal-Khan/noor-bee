When the sun is wrapped up,
and when the stars lose their light,
and when the mountains are moved away,
and when the (precious) pregnant camels are left untended,
and when the wild animals are gathered together,
and when the seas are set on fire,
and when the souls are paired (with their like),
and when the girl who was buried alive is asked,
for what sin she was killed,
and when the records of deeds are spread open,
and when the sky is stripped away,
and when Hell is set ablaze,
and when Paradise is brought near,
then every soul will know what it has brought.
So no! I swear by the stars that draw back,
that run their course and hide,
and by the night as it departs,
and by the morning as it breathes (brightens),
surely this (Quran) is the word brought by a noble messenger (Jibril),
full of strength, high in rank with the Owner of the Throne (Allah),
obeyed there, and trustworthy.
And (O people!) your companion (Muhammad ﷺ) is not mad.
He surely saw him (the angel) on the clear horizon.
And he does not hold back what he is told of the unseen.
And this is not the word of a cursed devil.
So where are you going?
It is only a reminder for all the worlds,
for any of you who wants to go straight.
And you cannot will anything unless Allah wills, the Lord of all the worlds.
