(O Prophet ﷺ) Read in the name of your Lord who created (everything).
He created man from a clinging clot (of blood).
Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous,
who taught by means of the pen,
taught man what he did not know.
No indeed! Surely man transgresses,
when he sees himself free of need (rich).
Surely to your Lord is the return (of everyone).
Have you seen the one who forbids
a servant when he prays?
Tell me, if he (the servant) is on guidance,
or commands fear of Allah (how can he be stopped)?
Tell me, if he (the one who forbids) denies and turns away,
does he not know that Allah sees?
No indeed! If he does not stop, We will drag him by the forelock,
a lying, sinful forelock.
Then let him call his gathering;
We will call the angels (of Hell).
No indeed! Do not obey him; prostrate and come near (to Allah).
