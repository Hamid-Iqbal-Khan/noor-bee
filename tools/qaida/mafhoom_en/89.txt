By the dawn,
and by the ten nights,
and by the even and the odd,
and by the night as it passes!
Is there not in these an oath enough for one who understands?
Have you not seen what your Lord did with the people of 'Aad,
the people of Iram with lofty pillars,
the like of whom (in strength) was never created in the lands?
And with Thamud, who carved out the rocks in the valley?
And with Pharaoh, the owner of the stakes?
All of them transgressed in the lands,
and spread much corruption there,
so your Lord poured on them the scourge of punishment.
Surely your Lord is ever watchful.
As for man, when his Lord tests him by honouring him and blessing him, he says: My Lord has honoured me.
But when He tests him by limiting his provision, he says: My Lord has humiliated me.
No indeed! Rather, you do not honour the orphan,
and you do not urge one another to feed the poor,
and you devour inheritance greedily,
and you love wealth very much.
No indeed! When the earth is pounded to dust,
and your Lord comes (as befits His majesty), with the angels in row after row,
and Hell is brought near that Day; that Day man will understand, but what use is understanding then?
He will say: I wish I had sent something ahead for this life of mine!
So that Day no one will punish as Allah punishes,
and no one will bind as He binds.
(It will be said to the good soul:) O soul at peace!
Return to your Lord, pleased with Him and pleasing to Him,
so enter among My (righteous) servants,
and enter My Paradise.
