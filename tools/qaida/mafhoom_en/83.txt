Woe to those who give short measure,
who take full measure when they take from people,
but give less when they measure or weigh for them.
Do they not think that they will be raised again
for a tremendous Day,
the Day all people will stand before the Lord of all the worlds?
No indeed! The record of the wicked is in "Sijjeen".
And what will make you know what Sijjeen is?
It is a written record.
Woe that Day to the deniers,
who deny the Day of Judgement;
and no one denies it except every sinful transgressor.
When Our verses are recited to him, he says: These are tales of the people of old.
No indeed! Rather, their evil deeds have covered their hearts with rust.
No indeed! That Day they will be kept from (seeing) their Lord,
then they will surely enter Hell,
then it will be said to them: This is what you used to deny.
No indeed! The record of the righteous is in "'Illiyyeen".
And what will make you know what 'Illiyyun is?
It is a written record,
witnessed by those brought near to Allah.
Surely the righteous will be in bliss,
seated on couches, looking (at everything).
You will see in their faces the brightness of bliss.
They will be given a pure, sealed drink,
whose seal is musk; so for this let those who compete, compete.
And it will be mixed with "Tasneem",
a spring from which those brought near to Allah drink.
Surely the criminals used to laugh at the believers (in the world),
and wink at one another when they passed by them,
and return to their families joking,
and when they saw them they said: These people are surely astray.
Yet they were not sent as guardians over them.
So today the believers are laughing at the disbelievers,
seated on couches, looking.
Have the disbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?
